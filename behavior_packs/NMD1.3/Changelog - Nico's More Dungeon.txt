Nico's More Dungeon

[Version: 1.3.40]
NOTE:
• The add-on will now only work with Minecraft versions 1.21.70 and higher.

          "General"
• Updated for compatibility with Minecraft version 1.21.71.
• Rearranged the custom items in creative inventory by adding them in new item groups.

          "New Additional"
BLOCKS:
• Added the Boss Trophies.
  - Types and qualities of trophies:
    - Brute Zombie Trophies: Stone, Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond
    - Rat Curserer Trophies: Stone, Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond
    - Elder Golem Trophies: Stone, Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond
    - Piglin Chief Trophies: Stone, Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond
  - Stone Boss Trophies are dropped by their respective bosses upon defeat.
  - Higher-quality trophies can be crafted by combining lower-quality ones.

ITEMS:
• Added the Potion of Brute, which can be obtained by brewing Rotten Brute Flesh in a Water Bottle. Consuming it grants 10 minutes of Strength II and 2 minutes of Speed I.

WEAPON EFFECTS:
• Magical Scourge: Weapons with this effect deal an extra 2 damage against magical-type mobs.
• Spirit Piercer: Weapons with this effect can damage spirit-type mobs with physical immunity.
• Earth Bane: Weapons with this effect deal an extra 2 damage against earth-elemental-type mobs.

          "Fixes & Changes"
MOBS:
• Bosses will now always drop a Stone Trophy upon defeat.
• Rat Curserer:
  - Rat Curserer will now take 2.0× damage from any Magic Missile.
• Elder Golem:
  - It will now take less damage from lava.
• Piglin Chief:
  - Fixed the avoidance behavior toward the Soul Fire Bird and Nether Soul Wisp.
  - It will no longer sit down in boats.
• Rat: 
  - Wild rats now have an increased success rate of destroying crops.
• Giant Rat & Cursed Rat:
  - Tamed giant rat health value has increased.
  - Wild Giant Rats and Cursed Rats can now also pick up and eat Rotten Brute Flesh.
  - Wild Giant Rats and Cursed Rats now have an increased success rate of destroying crops.
  
ITEMS:
• Fixed a bug where custom tools and weapons would take durability damage after digging certain instant-diggable blocks, such as Eyeblossom and Hanging Pale Moss.
• Magic Staffs:
  - Magic staff's magic missiles will now have a impact particles.
  - Magic staffs will now only take 2 durability damage when using them to break a block or as a melee weapon.
  - Magic staffs will no longer shoot projectiles when the user is crouching.
  - Curserer Staff: 
    - It now has a weapon effect called Magical Scourge & Spirit Piercer.
    - Curse Magic Missiles now deal area damage in a 3-block radius, inflicting 3 damage or 4 damage if the Book of Curses is equipped.
    - Durability value has increased to 128.
    - Cooldown duration has been reduced.
  - Piglin Fire Staff
    - Fire Spike Magic Missiles now deal splash damage in a 1-block radius, inflicting 2 damage.
    - Cooldown duration has been reduced.
    - Projectile range has increased.
• Piglin War Hammer:
  - The Piglin Sledge Hammer is now renamed to Piglin War Hammer.
  - It now has a weapon effect called Earth Bane at level 3.
  - Improved hit sound effects; the sound only plays when the user does not have the Weakness effect.
  - Increased the level of the applied Weakness effect.
