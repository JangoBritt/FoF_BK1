Nico's More Dungeon:

[Version: 1.5]
NOTE:
- If you’re updating from an older version, this add-on will not work properly in existing worlds.

          "General"
- Changed file identifiers.
- Shortened several file names.
- Added PBR texture support for all the custom blocks.
- Added language support for Spanish and Japanese.
- Revamped the structure chest and barrel loot tables, reducing the chances of some uncommon items.

MOBS:
- Leashable mobs can now properly be leashed to other mobs.
- Rat Curserer:
  - Reduced the cooldown of projectile missiles on ranged attack.
- Elder Golem:
  - Increased the damage dealt by ground spikes and made it capable of attacking other villager-type mobs.
- Piglin Chief:
  - Reduced the cooldown of projectile missiles on ranged attack.
- Shulkore:
  - Increased the damage dealt by its projectiles.
  - Increased the regeneration effect level when its box lids are closed.
- Cobbled Deepslate Golem:
  - Can now be leashed.

ITEMS:
- Curserer Staff:
  - Increased durability from 128 to 256.
  - Reduced cooldown from 1.5 to 1.2.
  - Increased projectile velocity.
- Piglin Fire Staff:
  - Reduced projectile damage from 7 to 6.
  - Reduced cooldown from 1.2 to 0.8.
  - Increased durability from 192 to 288.
  - Increased projectile velocity.
- Written Guide Books have been updated:
  - Curserer Staff
  - Piglin Fire Staff
  - Shulkore
